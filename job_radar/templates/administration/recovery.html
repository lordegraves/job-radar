Backup and recovery

Backups protect Junior profiles, companies, settings, the database, résumés, reports, and sanitized logs. Passwords remain in Windows Credential Manager or their configured environment variable and are never copied into a backup.

Create a restorable backup

Junior creates and verifies a private backup without changing active data.

Export readable data

Download a JSON record of Junior's database. This is useful for review or portability, but it cannot replace a restorable backup.

Restore a backup

Restoring replaces current Junior data. Junior first creates a separate safety backup of the current state.
