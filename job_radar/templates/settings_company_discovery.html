External company lookup

What this optional setting does

Junior first checks the public careers address you provide, follows its public links, and tries its built-in collectors. When this setting is enabled, Junior may ask Bing for possible official careers sites only after all of those local checks fail.

Bing receives a search phrase containing only the submitted company name, its public domain, and the words official careers jobs, plus format=rss. Bing can also observe your IP address and ordinary connection details.

Junior does not send profile or résumé information, desired roles, locations, application history, contact details, database contents, or submitted URL paths and query parameters. Search results are not trusted: Junior validates every candidate with its normal collector before saving a company. Failed candidates and search responses are discarded.

Advantages

  • May locate a job system that is separated from the company's public careers page.
  • Can reduce the need to find and paste a more specific job-search address.

Privacy and reliability tradeoffs

  • Bing can observe your IP address, the public company name and domain, and ordinary connection details.
  • Bing is an external service that Junior does not control and it may change or become unavailable.
  • External results are never trusted until Junior validates the source independently.

This setting affects company setup only. It is never used during normal scans, and Junior remains fully usable while it is disabled.

Off is the recommended default. Changing this does not alter existing companies or previously collected jobs.
