External company lookup

{% if discovery_form.external_lookup_enabled %}Enabled{% else %}Off (recommended){% endif %}

Junior first checks the public careers address and its built-in collectors. If those checks fail, this optional setting permits a limited Bing search for possible official careers sites.

What Bing receives

Bing receives a search phrase containing only the submitted company name, its public domain, and the words official careers jobs.

  • The submitted company name and public domain.
  • The words official careers jobs.
  • Your IP address and ordinary connection details.

Junior never sends profile, résumé, application, database, contact, or submitted URL-path information.

Safety boundary

Bing results are never accepted as proof. Junior validates every candidate using its normal collector before saving a company, then discards failed candidates and search responses.

This setting is never used during normal scans.
