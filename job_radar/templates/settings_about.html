What Junior does

The purpose and boundaries of the application.

Junior scans the public career sites of companies you select, compares jobs with your profile and résumé, and organizes the results.

  • Junior explains why a job was surfaced, the résumé strengths it found, and the largest material gaps.
  • Junior does not apply for jobs, contact employers, scrape LinkedIn, or bypass sign-in pages.
  • AI résumé tailoring is under development and unavailable. Scans and recommendations use Junior's tested local rules.
Next: Getting started

Getting started

Set up a profile, résumé, preferences, and companies.
  1. Create or select a profile under Profile / Resume.
  2. Add the résumé Junior should use and review your target roles, locations, workplace choices, compensation, schedule, travel, and work restrictions.
  3. Open Companies. Enter a name only to select a company already in Junior's local catalog. To discover and add a new company, paste any official company URL; a homepage, careers page, department page, search page, or public job posting is acceptable.
  4. Use each company's connection test if you want to confirm its source before a full scan.

Junior follows bounded official pages, cross-domain recruiting links, browser-loaded careers modules, and supported public job sitemaps. It identifies the recruiting platform, proves its result pagination, and saves the company only after the source returns actual current jobs. You do not need to hunt for the recruiting platform or its internal job-search URL.

If the live employer page explicitly has no openings, or its retained postings have passed their application deadlines, Junior reports No current jobs and saves nothing. Submit the same official URL again when the employer is hiring.

Walmart is profile-aware: Junior requests only Walmart-family jobs matching the active profile's target roles and preferred locations instead of downloading its entire public catalog. A successful Walmart connection test with no profile matches remains healthy and does not mean Walmart has no openings.

Each managed profile keeps its own preferences, résumé, reviewed jobs, applications, and history.

Scans and recommendations

How Junior collects, evaluates, and sorts jobs.

Junior collects public listings, normalizes different company formats into the same job record, removes clearly unrelated or ineligible work, and evaluates plausible jobs more deeply.

  • Top Match: strong fit with practical eligibility confirmed.
  • Potential Top Match: strong fit, but an important practical fact still needs confirmation.
  • Needs your review: possibly useful, but the evidence is incomplete or mixed.
  • Not actionable: unrelated, outside your stated requirements, already handled, or missing too many required qualifications.

If an employer publishes duties and preferred qualifications but does not state any required qualifications, Junior reports that résumé gaps could not be verified. It does not treat missing requirements as proof that there are no gaps, and it does not turn preferred qualifications into mandatory ones.

Later scans reuse unchanged job details where safe. When Junior's understanding of a recruiting platform changes, it automatically refreshes affected cached details on the next scan instead of continuing to use an older summary. Diagnostics reports collector problems without exposing private profile or résumé contents.

Reviewing and tracking jobs

Turn scan results into deliberate decisions.
  • Save for later keeps a job without claiming you applied.
  • Pass records your decision and prevents that exact posting from returning.
  • I applied moves the job into Active Applications.
  • Active Applications tracks follow-ups and outcomes; completed applications move into permanent Application History.

Notes and personal decisions do not change Junior's scoring rules.

Settings and backups

Email, scheduling, retention, backup, and recovery.
  • Settings controls report retention, optional email delivery, USAJOBS API access, scan scheduling, and application preferences.
  • Profile / Resume can transfer profile configuration without résumés or job activity.
  • Companies can append public collector definitions without replacing the receiving catalog or changing profile assignments.
  • New and upgraded installations receive a 50-employer starter catalog. None are selected automatically, and removing one does not cause Junior to restore it later.
  • Administration contains full backup, restore, export, and other installation-wide controls.
  • A Junior backup includes application-owned settings and data, but never operating-system credentials.
  • Updates and repairs replace application files while preserving user-owned data.

Privacy and safety

What stays local and what may leave the computer.
  • Your profile, résumé, decisions, applications, history, reports, and database remain in Junior's local user-data directory.
  • Company scans request public career-site information. Employers and their service providers can see ordinary network connection details.
  • For Walmart, Junior sends the active profile's target-role and preferred-location search terms to Walmart's public careers service. It does not send the profile name, rÃ©sumÃ©, skills, compensation, exclusions, applications, history, or credentials.
  • USAJOBS organizations require API access. Add the registered email and authorization key under Settings → USAJOBS API access. Junior stores the key only in the operating-system credential manager.
  • Email sends a generated report only when you configure and request it.
  • Company setup reads only the public company pages you submit and the public job-platform pages they advertise.
  • Never send a résumé, database, password, access token, or API key with a normal support request.

Troubleshooting

Where to look when something does not work.
  1. Open Diagnostics for a safe health summary and source warnings.
  2. Use Companies to test one employer or Scan to retry selected companies.
  3. After installing an update that improves a collector, run a new scan so Junior can refresh affected job details and recommendations.
  4. Download only Junior's sanitized diagnostic information when support requests it.

For an unexpected problem that continues, contact claytonmgraves@outlook.com. Do not include private job-search data or credentials.
